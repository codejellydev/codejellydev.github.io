## English

CodeJelly ("the developer") provides this privacy policy for the mobile game BounceOrBreak ("the app").

### 1. Personal data we collect

The app does not collect personal data. It does not require or store accounts, logins, names, email addresses, or location.

The app stores game data such as your best score and settings only on your device. The developer cannot access this data.

The app does not use third-party SDKs such as advertising, analytics, or social login. The app does not communicate over the network.

The app does not collect personal data from users of any age.

If third-party services such as advertising are added in the future, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Changes will be posted on this page.
